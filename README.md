## What is this?

This is a fan-made VR mod for **ReRAC**, the unofficial PC port of the PlayStation 2 classic *Ratchet & Clank*.

It's the real game: the same planets, story, enemies, weapons, vendors, bolts and gold bolts. What changes is that you
play it with a VR headset, through Ratchet's own eyes, and you use your actual hands. You swing the wrench, throw the
bombs, aim the guns, grab the ledges and swim with your arms.

## Thank you, ReRAC

None of this would exist without the [**ReRAC**](https://github.com/re-rac/rerac) team. They rebuilt Ratchet &
Clank as a native PC game, piece by piece, so it runs without an emulator. This mod only adds the VR layer on top of
their work.

Go check them out, follow their progress and support them: [re-rac.github.io](https://re-rac.github.io) ·
[Discord](https://discord.gg/v2Ek44kdyu).

Please **don't report VR bugs to the ReRAC team**. They have nothing to do with this mod. Report them
[here](https://github.com/npiriou/Rerac-vr/issues) instead.

## What's different in VR

**You are Ratchet**

- Everything is in first person, at Ratchet's eye height. You see his gloves where your hands are.
- Walk around your room and Ratchet walks with you. Use the stick to go further.
- Cutscenes play from Ratchet's eyes, and you can look around freely.
- You take off and land on planets from inside the ship's cockpit.
- Clank and Giant Clank sections are in first person too, with Clank's own hands.

**Fighting with your hands**

- **The wrench is a real swing.** Hold it and hit things. Weak taps don't count, so put some energy into it.
- **Throw the wrench** by letting go mid-swing. It flies where you threw it and comes back.
- **Hyper Strike:** in the air, slam the wrench downward.
- **Guns aim where your hand points.** Big weapons (Devastator, R.Y.N.O., Suck Cannon…) can be held with both hands.
- **Bombs, mines and Decoys are really thrown.** Hold the trigger, make a throwing motion, let go.
- **The Walloper** works with an actual punch.
- **Clank fights with his fists:** punch for real.
- Turrets are aimed with your hand.

**Moving around with your body**

- **Grab ledges with your hands.** Catch the edge when you fall, slide your hands along it, then push both hands
  down to climb up.
- **Swim with your arms,** breaststroke style.
- **Turn bolt cranks for real:** put the wrench on the bolt, hold it and walk around it.
- **Plug the Hydrodisplacer and Trespasser** into their slots by pushing them in.
- **Ride the hoverboard by leaning** left and right (or use the stick).
- On grind rails, the view follows the rail smoothly to stay comfortable.

**Menus and feel**

- The pause menu appears in front of you in the world. Point at an option and pull the trigger (or press A).
- Quick select: hold a button and reach toward the weapon you want.
- The Visibomb's camera view shows up on a little screen built into the weapon.
- On-screen hints show your controller's buttons instead of the PlayStation ones.
- Rumble for nearly everything: each weapon, hits, landings, and each of Clank's packs has its own feel.

## What you need

- **A Windows 10 or 11 PC** with a graphics card that can run VR.
- **A PC VR headset that works with SteamVR:** PICO, Meta Quest (with Link, Air Link or Virtual Desktop), Valve
  Index or Windows Mixed Reality.
- **[SteamVR](https://store.steampowered.com/app/250820/SteamVR/)**, installed and set as your OpenXR runtime
  (SteamVR → Settings → OpenXR).
- **Your own copy of Ratchet & Clank (PS2)** as a disc image (`.iso`). For now, only the original US release
  (SCUS-97199) works. No game files are included here, and none will ever be.

## How to play

1. Download the **`RatchetVR` zip file** from the [latest release](https://github.com/npiriou/Rerac-vr/releases/latest).
2. Unzip it somewhere easy, like your Desktop or Documents (not in Program Files).
3. Turn on your headset and start SteamVR.
4. Double-click **`RatchetVR.exe`**.
5. The first time, pick your `.iso` file. The game is copied from it once: it takes a few minutes and about 5 GB of
   disk space.

That's it. Next time, just steps 3 and 4. Your progress saves like in the original game.

> [!TIP]
> Windows may say "Windows protected your PC" the first time, because the game isn't signed. Click **More info**,
> then **Run anyway**.

**Just want to mess around?** Double-click **`RatchetVR Sandbox.exe`** instead. It drops you on planet Kerwan with
every weapon, gadget and planet unlocked, and full ammo. Sandbox saves are kept apart from your real save.

**New version out?** Unzip it, then move the `data` and `saves` folders from your old game folder into the new one.

## Controls

Button names are for Meta Quest / PICO controllers. On other controllers, use the matching button in the same spot.

### Moving

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| Walk | Left stick (or walk around your room) |
| Turn | Right stick left / right (turns in steps) |
| Jump / double jump | **A** |
| Crouch | Hold **left trigger** |
| Long jump / high jump | Hold **left trigger** + **A** |
| Side flip / back flip | Flick the left stick sideways or back + **A** while holding **left trigger** |
| Hover / strafe with Clank's packs, Hydro-Pack boost | Hold **X** or **B** |
| Grab a ledge | Squeeze a **grip** near the edge as you fall |
| Climb up a ledge | Push both hands down hard (or press **A**) |
| Let go of a ledge | Release both grips |
| Swim | Breaststroke with your hands. Stroke upward to dive |

### Fighting

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| Hold the wrench | Hold **right grip** |
| Hit | Swing for real |
| Throw the wrench | Let go of the grip mid-swing. It comes back |
| Put the wrench away | Let go slowly |
| Hyper Strike | Swing the wrench down hard while in the air |
| Shoot / use a gadget | **Right trigger**, aim with your right hand |
| Throw a bomb, mine or Decoy | Hold **right trigger**, make a throwing motion, let go. Let go slowly to cancel |
| Hold a big weapon with two hands | Squeeze **left grip** near the front of the weapon |
| Back to your weapon after using the wrench | **Right trigger** |

### Weapons, menus and the rest

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| Talk / interact | Click the **right stick** |
| Quick select | Hold the **right stick** click, move your right hand toward a weapon, let go |
| Next quick-select page | Pull **right trigger** while holding the right stick click |
| Pause | **Y** |
| Skip a cutscene | **Y** |
| Map | Click the **left stick** |
| Use a menu | Point with your right hand, then pull the **right trigger** or press **A**. **Y** goes back |
| Buy at a vendor | Browse with the right stick, **right trigger** to buy, right stick click to leave |
| Turn a bolt crank | Hold the wrench on the bolt with **right grip** and walk around it |
| Recenter the view | **F8** on the keyboard |

### Hoverboard

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| Steer | Lean left / right, or left stick |
| Boost | **Left trigger** |
| Shoot | **Right trigger** |
| Jump | **A** |
| Tricks | In the air, push the right stick in any direction (or hold left grip, X or B) |
| Spin | In the air, left stick |

### Clank and Giant Clank

| Action | How |
| --- | --- |
| Punch | Punch for real, with either hand |
| Give orders to the little robots / Giant Clank's beam | Click the **right stick** |
| Giant Clank's missiles | **Right trigger** |

A normal gamepad or keyboard still works too, if you prefer.

## VR Options

Open **Options → VR Options** from the main menu or the pause menu:

| Option | What it does | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Helmet | See Ratchet's helmet around your view when he wears one | On |
| Boots | See Ratchet's boots when you look down | On |
| Camera flip | Your view spins with Ratchet's flips. Only for very strong stomachs! | Off |
| Direction | What "forward" on the stick means: where your **body** faces, or where you **gaze** | Body |
| Hoverboard lean | Steer the hoverboard by leaning | On |
| Player body | See the body you play (Ratchet, Clank, Giant Clank or the Hologuise disguise) when you look down, without its head: **Cutscenes** in scenes and ship sequences, **Always** also while you play. **Off** still shows Ratchet's hands in scenes | Off |

**Port Options** (right above) has the graphics settings. VR starts on the **High** preset. A new game starts with the helpdesk (the tips voice and text) off; turn it on in **Options**.

## Comfort tips

- Play standing, with some free space around you. You will swing your arms!
- Turning happens in steps, to avoid motion sickness. If you feel sick, take a break: your body gets used to VR over a
  few sessions.
- If your view ends up in the wrong place, press **F8** to recenter it.
